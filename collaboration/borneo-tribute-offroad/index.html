BORNEO TRIBUTE OFFROAD

SOLD OUT • MAY 20 - JUNE 4 2023

Join us for a 4X4 Land Rover expedition through the most rugged and fascinating regions in the tropical belt. Welcome to the Borneo Tropical Jungle.

Enquire similar trip

Nature. Culture. Adventure.

20TH MAY - 4TH JUNE

After a huge success in safely completing 1000 miles across the Sumatra region of Indonesia, our next journey is to celebrate the beauty of the Borneo island.

  • More than 800km journey across Eastern and Central Borneo, our expedition will include approximately 400km of off-road trails, as well as opening new routes that have carefully been mapped by our experts. Get ready to explore some of island’s most unchartered territories.

  • We'll start off and end our trip from Balikpapan, the richest and most cosmopolitan city in the Borneo Island. We have also handpicked some of the most charming towns to conduct our stopovers along the expeditions. Our itinerary has been designed to include enough time to explore some of the most unique local attractions in each city.

    The hotel during our overnight trip at Kpalimé is also included with breakfast.

  • We’ll take you to the Persocal Borneo. For three days, you will immerse in the lives of a local village dubbed as the ‘Freshwater Avatar’ community, we’ll shadow and witness their day-to-day lives, and move around in their traditional boat -which is the local main method of transportation.

  • Throughout our expeditions, we’ll stop by at some of the biggest highlights of Borneo Island. The equator. The Pesut Fish sanctuary. The Orangutan habitat. In addition to that, we’ll give you some free time to spend on your own in the Kalimantan jungle.

Borneo island

Welcome to the third-largest island in the world and the largest island in Asia, known for its dense rainforests, biodiversity, tropical islands and indigenous culture. But we’ll take you further to the Persocal Borneo. to deep-dive into the island's unchartered territory and immerse with the local tribes.

The land rover experience

For more than 70 years, Land Rover has challenged what's possible, on and off-Road, making it the perfect vehicle to challenge the terrains of Borneo island.

Tribute to camel trophy series

The world's greatest driving adventure since 1980

A series of Persocal trips across the Indonesian archipelago to retrack the legacy of Camel Trophy- dubbed as the 4X4 of Olympics, Land Rover's legendary race that traversed the world's toughest terrains. We have adapted the routes to include some Indonesia's most unchartered regions, nature and culture.

Action packed

And of course, lots of fun

From immersing in local villages, to exploring the unchartered territory of Borneo's jungle. You'll start-off getting carstruck, and come home with life changing memories and knowledge.

  • Comfort

    Designed with comfort, power and off road capability, our Land Rovers are equipped with high-end features such as leather seating and advanced navigation systems.

  • Safety

    Our Land Rovers are equipped with a range of features to help drivers navigate challenging terrain of Borneo safely. 

    In addition, this trip is led by a team of more than 10 experts who have led various 4X4 expeditions across Indonesia. The routes have been carefully been chosen and surveyed, with various exact routes to mitigate any risks. A medical team will also accompany our journey.

  • Ledendary

    Be a part of history as we experience the most reliable and capable off-road vehicles ever made. Land Rover's reputation was solidified by its involvement in the Camel Trophy, an annual off-road race sponsored by Camel cigarettes from 1980 to 2000. Starting in 1980, teams from around the world competed in grueling off-road stages on three continents. The Camel trophy made four stops in Indonesia. Our trip has been designed to both honor the prestige and history of the Camel Trophy, and to celebrate the beauty of Indonesia. Join us as we recreate history in Indonesia.
