About Lisbon

About Lisbon

With year-round sunshine, Lisbon is one of Europe’s brightest and most inspiring cities. Enjoy buzzing creative scene, startup energy, surf culture, and a relaxed lifestyle at an affordable cost.

Why you’ll love Lisbon

  • The happiest city in the world

    The Portuguese capital gets a whopping 2,801 hours of sunshine a year, plus locals work an average of 31 hours a week – meaning there’s plenty of time to catch those rays. And it’s apparently the second friendliest place, too.

    NATIONAL GEOGRAPHIC

  • The best place for digital nomads to live

    A value for money destination for those looking for remote work, especially when compared to big European cities like Barcelona and Paris. 

    BUSINESS INSIDER

  • One of Europe's biggest startup hubs

    The city is home to over 30 incubators and accelerators and nearly 50 co-working spaces.

    FORBES

  • The world's greatest places in 2022

    Peak-y beach breaks like Carcavelos, just ten minutes from the bustling, historically important city center.

    SURFER MAG

  • The 'It Place' to visit in Europe right now

    The city was founded circa 1200 B.C., making it older than Rome, and is steeped in history namely due to its roots as a thriving port.

    ARCHITECTURAL DIGEST

  • Lisbon's cuisine is an adventure

    A new contingent of chefs, both local and international, are cresting a wave of modern Portuguese cuisine.

    CONDÉ NAST TRAVELLER

  • One of Europe’s more affordable capitals

    Twenty euros or under will buy you a three-course meal with wine at most restaurants.

    THE NEW YORK TIMES

For one month, the city unfolds itself—and Lisbon Sabbatical brings you right in like a local Lisboeta.
